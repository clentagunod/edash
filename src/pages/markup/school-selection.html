Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

What is E-Dash used for?

E-Dash is a school-focused platform for organizing enrollment information and learner records, reviewing school data, preparing reports, and supporting related workflows such as learner cards.

Who can access a school’s E-Dash workspace?

Users sign in through the selected school workspace. Available information and actions depend on that school’s account setup and assigned access role. Contact your school administrator if you need access or help with your account.

What should I do if my school is not listed?

Use “Register your school” to send your initial school and contact details. The E-Dash team will review your request and email a link to complete your school profile. After you submit the full form, our developer manually prepares your database and workspace over several days. We will email you when your school is ready.
